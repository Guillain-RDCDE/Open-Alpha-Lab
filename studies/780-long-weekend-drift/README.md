## Verdict

| Axis | Stamp | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal — is the effect real?** | ![Weak](https://img.shields.io/badge/Weak-dab617?style=flat-square) | The pre-holiday drift is *directionally real and jackknife-stable* — the eve beats an ordinary day (+0.10%, hit 58%) and the 3-session run-up clears the placebo (+0.30%, *t* = +2.20, right-tail p = 0.014). But the **classic single-eve cut is insignificant** (*t* = +1.53, p = 0.13), the effect only reaches significance on the wider window, and the detector is well-powered at n = 190 — so this is a genuine-but-weak tilt, not a *t* ≥ 2.5 result. |
| **Tradability — survives costs/scale?** | ![Fragile](https://img.shields.io/badge/Fragile-dab617?style=flat-square) | Survives *gross* on the run-up (*t* = 2.2) with ample SPY capacity, but the single-day version is **zeroed by a 5 bps round-trip** and the run-up's net edge (+0.20% @ 5 bps, *t* = 1.46) is marginal and vanishes by 10 bps. A break-even-ish, cost-sensitive curiosity, not a bankable edge. |

> **In one sentence:** the famous pre-holiday drift is *faintly present in the right direction*
> — the 3-session run-up into a holiday beats the tape (+0.30%, placebo p = 0.014) — but the
> textbook single-eve version is statistically unremarkable (*t* = 1.53) and dies the moment
> you pay a spread, leaving a weak, cost-fragile echo of the old anomaly rather than a tradable
> edge.
