# References & literature map — Study 57 (Yield-Trap)

## The belief and the theory

- **Miller, M., & Modigliani, F. (1961).** *Dividend Policy, Growth, and the Valuation of Shares.*
  Journal of Business — dividend irrelevance: payout policy does not, by itself, create value.
- **Asness, C., Frazzini, A., & Pedersen, L. (2019).** *Quality Minus Junk* — high-quality (not
  high-yield) firms carry the premium; dividend yield is a noisy value/sector proxy.
- **Vendor / belief family** — dividend and income strategies; backlog:
  [`docs/pwb_strategies_inventory.md`](../../../docs/pwb_strategies_inventory.md).

## On "yield traps" and home-made dividends

- A high trailing yield often reflects a falling price; "make your own dividend" (selling shares) gives a
  total-return investor the same cash without the tilt. The study makes the total-return comparison explicit.

## Data

- **Yahoo! Finance** — VYM (Vanguard High Dividend Yield) and SPY, monthly auto-adjusted (total-return)
  prices, 2007–2026. The offline synthetic world injects a (counterfactual) dividend premium and a null.
