| **Signal — is the effect real?** | ![None](https://img.shields.io/badge/None-c0392b?style=flat-square) | Every real cut is indistinguishable from noise: run-up +0.22% (*t* +0.12) and +1.44% (*t* +0.60); reaction −1.16% (*t* −0.71) and −1.62% (*t* −0.93). All |
| **Tradability — survives costs/scale?** | ![Mirage](https://img.shields.io/badge/Mirage-c0392b?style=flat-square) | Nothing to bank. The run-up is a rounding error that vanishes after costs; the reaction is a sub-1-*t* negative. No edge in any window, gross or net. |

> **In one sentence:** a chart-smashing album drop lights up the streaming charts but leaves
> Spotify's *stock* untouched — the "buy SPOT into the drop" trade is folklore, because one
> record week is a rounding error against a subscription business the market has already
> priced.
