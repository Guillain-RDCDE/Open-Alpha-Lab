# Sources & literature map — Study 986 (The Rolling Ladder)

## Immunisation — where the crossover horizon comes from

- **Redington, F. M. (1952), "Review of the Principles of Life-Office Valuations", *Journal of
  the Institute of Actuaries* 78(3), 286-340.** The origin of immunisation: a portfolio whose
  duration equals the investment horizon is insulated from a parallel rate shift to first order.
  This study's crossover result is that theorem run backwards.
- **Fisher, L. & Weil, R. L. (1971), "Coping with the Risk of Interest-Rate Fluctuations",
  *Journal of Business* 44(4), 408-431.** Immunisation made operational, and the conditions
  under which it fails.
- **Bierwag, G. O., Kaufman, G. G. & Toevs, A. (1983), "Duration: Its Development and Use in
  Bond Portfolio Management", *Financial Analysts Journal* 39(4), 15-35.** The survey; in
  particular why a *rolling* portfolio's behaviour differs from a maturing one's.

## Constant-maturity portfolios specifically

- **Leibowitz, M. L., Bova, A. & Kogelman, S. (2014), "Long-Term Bond Returns under Duration
  Targeting", *Financial Analysts Journal* 70(1), 31-51.** The single closest paper to this
  study: it shows analytically that a duration-targeted portfolio's return converges to its
  starting yield over roughly **twice the duration minus one** years. Worth reading immediately
  after this notebook.
- **Leibowitz, M. L. & Bova, A. (2013), "Duration Targeting and Index Convergence", *Morgan
  Stanley Research*.** The companion piece with the simulation evidence.
- **Homer, S. & Leibowitz, M. L. (2013), *Inside the Yield Book*, 3rd ed., Wiley.** The standard
  reference for everything in section 1.

## The instruments

- **Ben-David, I., Franzoni, F. & Moussawi, R. (2017), "Exchange-Traded Funds", *Annual Review of
  Financial Economics* 9, 169-189.** What an ETF wrapper does and does not change.
- **Dickson, J. M. et al. (2015), "Bond ETFs: Benefits, Challenges, Opportunities", Vanguard
  Research.** Practitioner treatment of the roll, the fee and the tracking difference.
