# References & literature map — Study 62 (Premium-Seller)

## The product and the belief

- **Whaley, R. (2002).** *Return and Risk of CBOE Buy Write Monthly Index.* Journal of Derivatives —
  the BXM buy-write index: covered calls reduce volatility but cap upside; risk-adjusted gains are modest.
- **Israelov, R., & Nielsen, L. (2015).** *Covered Calls Uncovered.* Financial Analysts Journal —
  decomposes covered calls into equity + short-vol + a timing bet; the "income" is mostly equity beta
  given back, not alpha.
- **Vendor / income belief** — high-distribution covered-call ETFs (QYLD, JEPI); backlog:
  [`docs/pwb_strategies_inventory.md`](../../../docs/pwb_strategies_inventory.md).

## On "income" as an illusion

- **Miller, M., & Modigliani, F. (1961)** — payout form (here, option premium distributed) isn't a
  premium; total return is what matters. The same lesson as [Study 57 Yield-Trap](../../57-yield-trap/).

## Data

- **Yahoo! Finance** — QYLD, QQQ (its underlying), SPY, monthly total return, 2014–2026. The offline
  synthetic world caps the fund's up-moves (and a null with no cap) so the asymmetry is provable offline.

*An "income illusion" companion to [57 Yield-Trap](../../57-yield-trap/) (high dividend); contrast the
short-vol premium done honestly in [63 Free-Fall](../../63-free-fall/).*
