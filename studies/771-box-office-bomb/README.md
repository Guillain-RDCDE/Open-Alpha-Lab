## Verdict

| Axis | Stamp | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal — is the effect real?** | ![Weak](https://img.shields.io/badge/Weak-dab617?style=flat-square) | The sign is the folklore's: DIS under-performs SPY after a flop at both horizons (−1.03% / −1.77%), and the jackknife keeps *t* negative on every leave-one-out. But |
| **Tradability — survives costs/scale?** | ![Mirage](https://img.shields.io/badge/Mirage-c0392b?style=flat-square) | Nothing to bank. The short earns ~+0.9–1.7% per event with *t* ≈ 1.4–1.5 (insignificant), fires 16 times in 13 years, is borrow-/capacity-trivial, and sits inside a multi-window search. |

> **In one sentence:** selling Disney after a notorious flop points the right way — the stock
> does mildly lag the market in the weeks after a bomb — but the effect is too small and too
> noisy (|*t*| ≈ 1.5, placebo p ≈ 0.15) to distinguish from Disney's ordinary underperformance,
> and there is no tradable edge in a 16-event, once-a-year curiosity.
