## Verdict

| Axis | Stamp | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal — is the effect real?** | ![Weak](https://img.shields.io/badge/Weak-dab617?style=flat-square) | The run-up points the *right* way — XRT beats SPY by +1.33% into Black Friday, hits 13/20 years, sits in the placebo right tail (p ≈ 0.026), and the jackknife stays positive. That is a genuine, directionally-correct whiff — more than most calendar folklore delivers. But the cross-year *t* is only +1.59 (below significance), it **weakens** at a 1-month lookback, the "sell the news" fade is a clean zero, and the synthetic null fires |
| **Tradability — survives costs/scale?** | ![Mirage](https://img.shields.io/badge/Mirage-c0392b?style=flat-square) | Nothing to bank. Net of costs the run-up is +1.2%/yr with *t* ≈ 1.5 — one positive cut inside a four-window search, no reliable statistical edge, and its partner "fade" trade doesn't exist. Liquid and long (capacity is fine), but there is no dependable signal to size. |

> **In one sentence:** retail *does* drift up into Black-Friday week — by about +1.3% vs the
> market over the prior fortnight, the right direction and unusual vs random windows — but
> the effect is small, only marginally significant (*t* ≈ 1.6), fades at a longer lookback,
> and comes with no "sell the news" reversal, so it is a suggestive curiosity, not a
> bankable trade.
