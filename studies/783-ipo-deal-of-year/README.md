> **In one sentence:** the banks' "IPO of the year" does disappoint the *typical* buyer — 13 of
> 17 trail the index over their first year — but the basket mean is a statistical coin-flip
> (the pop makes 3-month returns *positive*, and a handful of lottery winners cancel the many
> losers), and since the crown is awarded ex post it was never a trade to begin with.
