## Verdict

| Axis | Stamp | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal — is the effect real?** | ![Weak](https://img.shields.io/badge/Weak-dab617?style=flat-square) | The clean, non-overlapping tests are quiet: the 1-week devil quintile is a dead zero (*t* = −0.22) and the 1-week LS spread is noise (*t* = +0.82, placebo p = 0.18). There is a *persistent folklore-signed lean* — LS positive at both horizons, hit rate ~54.5%, jackknife-stable positive — and the monthly spread even hits placebo p = 0.006, but that evaporates the moment you de-overlap the windows (non-overlapping monthly *t* = +1.42, p = 0.06). Borderline, horizon-fragile, and — since a stock's *nominal* mantissa is economically meaningless (split history, not fundamentals) — mechanism-free. |
| **Tradability — survives costs/scale?** | ![Mirage](https://img.shields.io/badge/Mirage-c0392b?style=flat-square) | Nothing to bank. Even the overlap-inflated edge is ~18-25 bps/month gross on a weekly-turnover long/short of ~20 names a side sorted on an arbitrary nominal price digit; net of costs and once de-overlapped it isn't significant. No mechanism, no clean significance, no capacity story. |

> **In one sentence:** the "number of the beast" price sort shows a faint, folklore-signed
> lean (clean names edge out the 666-touchers) that looks significant only through a
> window-overlap illusion and vanishes under a clean non-overlapping test — a Weak,
> mechanism-free curiosity and an untradeable Mirage.
