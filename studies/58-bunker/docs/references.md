# References & literature map — Study 58 (Bunker)

## The effect and its tradable form

- **Baker, M., Bradley, B., & Wurgler, J. (2011).** *Benchmarks as Limits to Arbitrage: Understanding
  the Low-Volatility Anomaly.* Financial Analysts Journal — the canonical low-vol anomaly.
- **Blitz, D., & van Vliet, P. (2007).** *The Volatility Effect.* Journal of Portfolio Management —
  low-risk stocks earn higher risk-adjusted returns; the basis for min-vol products.
- **Open-Alpha-Lab [Study 18 Dull-Roar](../../18-dull-roar/)** — the desk's academic low-vol long-short;
  this study tests the *tradable product* (USMV) net of fees.

## On products vs anomalies and the regime caveat

- **Asness, C., Frazzini, A., & Pedersen, L. (2014).** *Low-Risk Investing Without Industry Bets* — much
  of the low-vol effect is industry/leverage; product implementations vary.
- The 2011–2026 sample is bull-dominated; min-vol's edge is concentrated in bear markets and full cycles
  — the honest regime caveat in the verdict.

## Data

- **Yahoo! Finance** — USMV (iShares MSCI USA Min Vol) and SPY, monthly total return, 2011–2026
  (USMV's inception). The offline synthetic world gives the min-vol sleeve beta<1 (lower vol) and a
  tunable alpha (and a null).
