# Sources & literature map — Study 977 (Maximum Diversification)

## The method

- **Choueifaty, Y. & Coignard, Y. (2008), "Toward Maximum Diversification", *Journal of
  Portfolio Management* 35(1), 40-51.** The diversification ratio, the most diversified
  portfolio, and the argument that it is the maximum-Sharpe portfolio when expected returns are
  proportional to volatility.
- **Choueifaty, Y., Froidure, T. & Reynier, J. (2013), "Properties of the Most Diversified
  Portfolio", *Journal of Investment Strategies* 2(2), 49-70.** The formal properties, including
  the "core properties" the MDP satisfies and minimum variance does not.
- **Clarke, R., de Silva, H. & Thorley, S. (2013), "Risk Parity, Maximum Diversification, and
  Minimum Variance: An Analytic Perspective", *Journal of Portfolio Management* 39(3),
  39-53.** The paper that puts all three in one algebraic frame and shows exactly when they
  coincide — the source of this study's degenerate-case test.

## The competitors

- **Maillard, S., Roncalli, T. & Teïletche, J. (2010), *Journal of Portfolio Management* 36(4),
  60-70.** Equal risk contribution.
- **DeMiguel, V., Garlappi, L. & Uppal, R. (2009), *Review of Financial Studies* 22(5),
  1915-1953.** 1/N, the benchmark that refuses to lose.
- **Jagannathan, R. & Ma, T. (2003), *Journal of Finance* 58(4), 1651-1683.** Why the long-only
  constraint is itself a form of shrinkage — relevant because every portfolio here is long-only.

## Scepticism

- **Lee, W. (2011), "Risk-Based Asset Allocation: A New Answer to an Old Question?", *Journal of
  Portfolio Management* 37(4), 11-28.** Risk-based schemes differ less from one another than
  their marketing suggests, and most of their common advantage is a low-volatility tilt.
- **Scherer, B. (2011), "A Note on the Returns from Minimum Variance Investing", *Journal of
  Empirical Finance* 18(4), 652-660.** How much of a risk-based portfolio's performance is
  explained by known factor exposures rather than by the construction.
- **Ledoit, O. & Wolf, M. (2003, 2004).** Any of these methods is only as good as the covariance
  matrix it is given — study **975** on this desk.
