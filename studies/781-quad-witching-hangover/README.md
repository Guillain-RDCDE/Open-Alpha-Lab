## Verdict

| Axis | Stamp | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal — is the effect real?** | ![None](https://img.shields.io/badge/None-c0392b?style=flat-square) | The week after quad-witching returns **+0.08%** (*t* = +0.28), *positive*, not a hangover; the 2-week window is +0.38% (*t* = +0.94); placebo left-tail p = 0.25 / 0.36 (dead centre); jackknife *t* pinned to zero (−0.23…+0.59). Indistinguishable from noise, and the sign is opposite the folklore. |
| **Tradability — survives costs/scale?** | ![Mirage](https://img.shields.io/badge/Mirage-c0392b?style=flat-square) | Nothing to bank. "Shorting the hangover" shorts a *positive* window and loses gross; costs make the 1-week leg negative outright. No edge long or short. |

> **In one sentence:** the "quad-witching hangover" is a myth — SPY's week after quarterly
> quad-witching is mildly *positive* (+0.08%, *t* = +0.28), sits dead-centre of the
> random-window placebo, and offers no tradable edge in either direction.
