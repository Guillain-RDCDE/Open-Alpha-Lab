# Sources & literature map — Study 983 (The Weekend Oracle)

## Lead-lag and the non-synchronous-trading trap

- **Scholes, M. & Williams, J. (1977), "Estimating Betas from Nonsynchronous Data", *Journal of
  Financial Economics* 5(3), 309-327.** The founding statement of the problem this study spends
  its first section on: when two assets' closes are not simultaneous, measured lead-lag is
  partly an artefact of the clock.
- **Lo, A. W. & MacKinlay, A. C. (1990), "An Econometric Analysis of Nonsynchronous Trading",
  *Journal of Econometrics* 45(1-2), 181-211.** How much spurious autocorrelation and
  cross-correlation non-synchronous closes generate, quantified.
- **Chordia, T. & Swaminathan, B. (2000), "Trading Volume and Cross-Autocorrelations in Stock
  Returns", *Journal of Finance* 55(2), 913-935.** Lead-lag between liquid and illiquid assets,
  and how much of it survives careful alignment.

## Information arriving while the market is shut

- **French, K. R. & Roll, R. (1986), "Stock Return Variances: The Arrival of Information and the
  Reaction of Traders", *Journal of Financial Economics* 17(1), 5-26.** The classic
  demonstration that variance accrues with *trading* time, not calendar time — the reason a
  65-hour closure is such a clean experiment.
- **Barclay, M. J. & Hendershott, T. (2003), "Price Discovery and Trading After Hours", *Review
  of Financial Studies* 16(4), 1041-1073.** How much price discovery happens when the primary
  market is closed.
- **Lou, D., Polk, C. & Skouras, S. (2019), "A Tug of War: Overnight Versus Intraday Expected
  Returns", *Journal of Financial Economics* 134(1), 192-213.** The overnight/intraday split
  that this study's weekend window is an extreme case of.

## Crypto and the risk-asset complex

- **Liu, Y. & Tsyvinski, A. (2021), "Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency", *Review of Financial
  Studies* 34(6), 2689-2727.** What does and does not price crypto returns, including its
  relationship with equity factors.
- **Iyer, T. (2022), "Cryptic Connections: Spillovers between Crypto and Equity Markets", IMF
  Global Financial Stability Note 2022/01.** Documents the post-2020 rise in crypto-equity
  co-movement that this study's regime split is built around.
- **Baur, D. G. & Dimpfl, T. (2021), "The Volatility of Bitcoin and its Role as a Medium of
  Exchange and a Store of Value", *Empirical Economics* 61, 2663-2683.** On Bitcoin's
  round-the-clock volatility profile.
