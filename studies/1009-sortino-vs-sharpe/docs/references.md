# Sources & literature map — Study 1009 (Sortino's Free Lunch)

## The ratio

- **Sortino, F. A. & Price, L. N. (1994), "Performance Measurement in a Downside Risk
  Framework", *Journal of Investing* 3(3), 59-64.** The definition used here, including the
  full-sample denominator convention that keeps downside deviation on the same scale as σ.
- **Sortino, F. A. & van der Meer, R. (1991), "Downside Risk", *Journal of Portfolio
  Management* 17(4), 27-31.**
- **Markowitz, H. (1959), *Portfolio Selection: Efficient Diversification of Investments*,
  Wiley, ch. IX.** Semivariance, and Markowitz's own reasons for preferring variance anyway —
  chiefly the estimation cost that section 4 measures.
- **Bawa, V. S. (1975), "Optimal Rules for Ordering Uncertain Prospects", *Journal of Financial
  Economics* 2(1), 95-121.** Lower partial moments and stochastic dominance.
- **Fishburn, P. C. (1977), "Mean-Risk Analysis with Risk Associated with Below-Target
  Returns", *American Economic Review* 67(2), 116-126.**

## Do downside measures rank differently?

- **Eling, M. & Schuhmacher, F. (2007), "Does the Choice of Performance Measure Influence the
  Evaluation of Hedge Funds?", *Journal of Banking & Finance* 31(9), 2632-2647.** Finds rank
  correlations near one across a dozen measures — the result section 2 reproduces on indices.
- **Eling, M. (2008), "Does the Measure Matter in the Mutual Fund Industry?", *Financial
  Analysts Journal* 64(3), 54-66.**
- **Zakamouline, V. (2011), "The Performance Measure You Choose Influences the Evaluation of
  Hedge Funds", *Journal of Performance Measurement* 15(3), 48-64.** The dissent: argues the
  agreement is an artefact of the samples used.

## Estimation error in performance ratios

- **Lo, A. W. (2002), "The Statistics of Sharpe Ratios", *Financial Analysts Journal* 58(4),
  36-52.** Standard errors for Sharpe, including under serial correlation.
- **Jobson, J. D. & Korkie, B. (1981), "Performance Hypothesis Testing with the Sharpe and
  Treynor Measures", *Journal of Finance* 36(4), 889-908.**
- **Ledoit, O. & Wolf, M. (2008), "Robust Performance Hypothesis Testing with the Sharpe
  Ratio", *Journal of Empirical Finance* 15(5), 850-859.** The bootstrap approach section 4
  borrows.
- **Kim, T.-H. & White, H. (2004), "On More Robust Estimation of Skewness and Kurtosis",
  *Finance Research Letters* 1(1), 56-73.** Why sample skewness is unreliable — the basis of
  section 5.
